Bird Songs

Most birders hear a bird long before they see it. Press play, then read what to listen for. Every recording is credited with its Creative Commons license.

Northern Cardinal

Listen for: Clear, falling whistles: cheer, cheer, cheer or birdie, birdie, birdie.

What you'll hear: A series of four loud, clear whistles that slide downward in pitch.

Carolina Wren

Listen for: A rolling teakettle, teakettle, teakettle.

What you'll hear: A fast, three-part phrase repeated four times, rising and falling.

Recording: placeholder, XC000000 by [recordist], CC BY-NC-SA 4.0

American Robin

Listen for: Cheerful, rising and falling phrases: cheerily, cheer-up, cheerio.

What you'll hear: Five short, whistled phrases that go up and down, with pauses between.

Recording: placeholder, XC000000 by [recordist], CC BY-NC-SA 4.0

Blue Jay

Listen for: A harsh, repeated jay, jay, jay.

What you'll hear: Three harsh, downward calls with short pauses between.

Recording: placeholder, XC000000 by [recordist], CC BY-NC-SA 4.0

Red-winged Blackbird

Listen for: A gurgling conk-la-ree! that ends in a long trill.

What you'll hear: Two short notes followed by a long, buzzy, high trill.
